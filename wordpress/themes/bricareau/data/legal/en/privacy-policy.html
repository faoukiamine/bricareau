2. Purpose of this policy

This privacy policy explains how Bricareau Water Specialist collects, uses, keeps, discloses and protects your personal information, in accordance with the obligations arising from:

  • Law 25 – An Act to modernize legislative provisions as regards the protection of personal information (Québec)
  • The Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA)
  • The Act respecting the protection of personal information in the private sector (Québec)

By accessing our website or using our services, you consent to the practices described in this policy.

3. Personal information collected

We may collect the following personal information:

3.1 Information you provide directly

  • First and last name
  • Full mailing address (including municipality and postal code)
  • Phone number
  • Email address
  • Information about your property (type of residence, water source, water quality problems)
  • Information needed to carry out a free water test or provide services

3.2 Information collected automatically

  • IP address
  • Browser type and operating system

  • Approximate location (region, city)
  • Website performance data (through analytics tools such as Google Analytics)

3.3 Information from third parties

  • Data from advertising platforms (Google Ads, Facebook/Meta Ads)
  • Information shared through our contact or appointment forms

4. Purposes of collection

Personal information is collected only for the following purposes:

  • Providing our residential water treatment services (softeners, filtration, reverse osmosis, etc.)
  • Scheduling and carrying out home visits and free water tests
  • Communicating with you to answer your requests for information or quotes
  • Sending you relevant information about our products and services (with your consent)
  • Improving our website and services by analyzing browsing data
  • Meeting our legal and regulatory obligations
  • Preventing fraud and keeping our computer systems secure

We only collect the information strictly necessary for the purposes above, in accordance with the principle of data minimization.

5. Legal basis for processing

The processing of your personal information is based on one or more of the following legal grounds:

  • Your express consent, obtained when you submit a form or book an appointment
  • The performance of a contract or of pre-contractual measures at your request
  • Compliance with a legal obligation that applies to Bricareau Water Specialist
  • The pursuit of our legitimate interests, with respect for your fundamental rights

In accordance with Law 25, you may withdraw your consent at any time, subject to legal or contractual restrictions.

7. Retention and destruction of information

Your personal information is kept only as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes for which it was collected, or for the periods required by law.

At the end of the retention period, personal information is securely destroyed or anonymized so that you can no longer be identified.

As a rule, we apply the following retention periods:

  • Active clients: length of the business relationship plus 3 years
  • Prospects (contact forms): 12 months from the last contact
  • Browsing data: 24 months maximum
  • Accounting and tax data: 7 years, in accordance with Canadian tax legislation

8. Your rights

In accordance with Law 25 and PIPEDA, you have the following rights regarding your personal information:

8.1 Right of access

You have the right to obtain a copy of the personal information we hold about you.

8.2 Right to rectification

If your information is inaccurate, incomplete or ambiguous, you can ask us to correct it.

8.3 Right to erasure

You can ask us to delete your personal information when keeping it is no longer justified, subject to our legal obligations.

8.4 Right to data portability

You have the right to receive the personal information you provided to us in a structured, commonly used technological format.

8.5 Right to withdraw consent

You can withdraw your consent at any time for processing based on it, without retroactive effect.

8.6 Right to object to marketing

You can unsubscribe from our marketing communications at any time by clicking the unsubscribe link in our emails or by contacting us directly.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at the email address given in section 1 of this policy. We will process your request within 30 days.

Our website uses cookies and similar technologies to improve your browsing experience and analyze our traffic.

10. Security of personal information

Bricareau Water Specialist implements appropriate technical and organizational security measures to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, disclosure, modification or accidental destruction, including:

  • Encryption of data in transit (SSL/TLS protocol)
  • Access to personal information restricted on a need-to-know basis
  • Secure passwords and two-factor authentication for internal systems
  • Regular updates of our systems and software
  • Training of our staff on the protection of personal information

In the event of a confidentiality incident (unauthorized access, use or disclosure) likely to cause serious harm, we will inform you and notify the Commission d'accès à l'information (CAI) du Québec, in accordance with Law 25.
